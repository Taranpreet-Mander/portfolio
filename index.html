About

I'm a 3rd-year B.E. Information Security student who learns best by breaking things in a safe lab and then figuring out how to detect them.

Most of my recent work is blue team — running attacks against my own VMs and catching them with a SIEM, then automating the response like a real SOC would. I also write small Python security tools to understand the internals instead of just using them.

I'm still early in my journey and I'm honest about that — my repos are works-in-progress with notes on what broke and what I learned. That's the point.
