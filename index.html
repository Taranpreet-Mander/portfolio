About Me

I'm Taranpreet Singh Mander, a 3rd-year B.E. Information Security student who transformed curiosity about how systems break into a commitment to making them stronger. My journey combines theoretical knowledge from coursework with hands-on experimentation and real-world application development.

I believe that security isn't an afterthought—it's architecture. My approach involves understanding threat models, implementing secure coding practices, and conducting thorough vulnerability assessments. I'm equally comfortable analyzing network traffic, writing intrusion detection logic, or architecting secure authentication systems.
